file3

Upload helper for one Japanese drama Shorts channel · updated 2026-11-10

file3 is a small personal tool. The channel owner signs in once with Google and allows it to place finished short videos on their own YouTube channel. Nobody else uses it.

What it does

  • Reads finished vertical videos from the owner's local folder.
  • Uses the title, description and tags written in a text file next to each video.
  • Uploads them as private or with a scheduled publish time chosen by the owner.
  • Marks every video as not made for kids and labels altered or synthetic content.
  • Checks the upload result and the channel's list of scheduled videos.

What it does not do

  • It does not change channel settings, monetization or subscriber data.
  • It does not sell, share or use data for advertising.
  • It does not post comments or contact viewers.
