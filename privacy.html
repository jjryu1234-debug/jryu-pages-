Privacy Policy

file3 · effective 2026-11-10

Data we access

With the owner's permission, file3 uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, read the channel's own video list and status, and update details of videos it uploaded. Permissions requested: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, youtube.

How it is stored

The access token is kept only on the owner's computer. Video files, titles and descriptions stay in the owner's local folders. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for ads. file3's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect file3 at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections. Local tokens are then deleted on request.
